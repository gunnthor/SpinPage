      SpinPage

      by Gunnþór Karl Rafnsson

      A dramatic spinning wheel and random name picker for draft orders, giveaways, party games, and any moment that deserves a little spectacle — with confetti, fanfare, plot twists, and just enough chaos to keep the table honest.

      Keyboard

      • Space spins the wheel when you're not typing.
      • Enter in the name box adds a name; paste a list to add many at once.
      • Esc closes any panel or dialog, and skips No One Is Safe or a Panic game to the result.

      Is it fair?

      The winner is drawn by your browser's random number generator the moment a spin starts, weighted by slice size. Power-ups change slice sizes and the real odds, and the new odds are shown. Hand flicks, wind-backs, the gremlin and pop-offs only change how the wheel gets there. About one spin in twenty is ruled illegal: the wheel is stamped, nobody is picked, and you spin again. The one exception is Rigged, which you have to switch on yourself.

      Built for the sacred moment when everyone says, “Just spin it already.”

      A spinning wheel that picks a name — and then a whole order

      SpinPage is a free online wheel spinner and random name picker. Type in the names, press SPIN, and the wheel lands on one of them. Where most wheel spinners stop there, SpinPage keeps going: the winner steps off the wheel and takes the next slot in the draft order, so spinning until the wheel is empty leaves you with a complete ranked list you can copy straight out. Nothing to install, no account, and no ads.

      How to use it

      1. Add the names

      Type a name and press Enter. Two is the minimum; there is no upper limit, and the wheel keeps subdividing as the list grows.

      2. Spin

      Press SPIN, or grab the wheel with the mouse or a finger, drag it round and let go. Your flick decides how fast it goes and how many laps it turns — never where it stops. Flick it hard enough and the wheel can come off its hub entirely, bouncing or rolling across the screen before it drops back on to finish the spin.

      3. Take the result

      The winner is announced, then leaves the wheel for the next pick in the draft order. Turn on autospin and the remaining rounds run themselves.

      What people use it for

      • Draft orders — fantasy leagues, five-a-side sides, tournament seeding, who picks first.
      • Random name picker for class — who answers, who presents, who goes next.
      • Giveaway and raffle draws — a visible, watchable draw instead of a number out of a script.
      • Deciding as a group — where to eat, who does the washing up, who takes the last shift.
      • Game night order — turn order, team splits, dramatic tie-breakers.

      Extras, if you want them

      • Power-up slices — Hot Seat, Cursed and Wildcard swell or shrink somebody's real odds, with the live percentage shown beside the wheel.
      • Panic — a hidden wedge that throws the drafted field into a horse race over two to four hurdles, or onto the slot machines, where any 7 sticks and the first to hit 7-7-7 are safe. Only half of them survive.
      • Wind-back — the wheel lands, settles, then gets shoved into a whole second spin the other way for a different answer.
      • Every extra is a switch in the options panel. Turn them all off and you have a plain, fast wheel spinner.

      Questions

      Is SpinPage free?

      Yes — free, no account, no ads. Open the page, add names, spin.

      Is the wheel actually random?

      Yes. The winner is drawn the moment you release the wheel, from the browser's random number generator. Spinning by hand changes the speed and the number of laps, but not where it lands, so the person spinning cannot steer it. The one exception is the Rigged option, which you have to switch on yourself.

      How many names can I add?

      Two at minimum, no fixed maximum. Names of up to 30 characters fit on a slice.

      Does it work on a phone?

      Yes. The layout adapts to phones and tablets, and the wheel takes touch — flick it with a finger.

      Does it save my names?

      Your names, draft order and settings live in your own browser's local storage, so they survive a refresh. Nothing is uploaded and no server holds your list.

      Can I get a full draft order rather than one winner?

      That is the default. Each winner leaves the wheel and takes the next pick, and COPY puts the finished order on your clipboard.

      Do I need to install anything?

      No. SpinPage is a single web page — it runs in the browser, offline once loaded, with no sign-up and no download.

      Can I undo a spin?

      Yes. UNDO reverses the last thing that happened — a pick, a stolen seat or a whole PANIC game. RESTORE ALL puts every pick back on the wheel, and DELETE PICKS takes the drafted players off the table; both can be taken back from the message that pops up.
